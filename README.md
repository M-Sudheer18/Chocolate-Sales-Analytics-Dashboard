# 🍫 Chocolate Sales Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This Power BI dashboard provides a comprehensive analysis of chocolate sales, profitability, shipment performance, product performance, geographic distribution, and salesperson contributions from January 2023 to March 2025. The dashboard helps stakeholders monitor key business metrics, identify sales trends, evaluate product profitability, and support data-driven decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Monitor overall sales, cost, profit, and shipment performance.
- Analyze product-wise revenue and profitability.
- Track shipment trends over time.
- Evaluate regional sales distribution.
- Identify top-performing salespersons.
- Support strategic business decisions through interactive visualizations.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### 1. Business KPIs
The dashboard displays major performance indicators including:

- Total Sales Amount: **$141.49M**
- Total Cost: **$60.39M**
- Total Profit: **$81M**
- Total Shipments: **25K**
- Profit Margin: **57.3%**
- Total Boxes Sold: **9M**
